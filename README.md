# 4D-gamelet

**Tether** — a 4D viewer and gamelet. Open `index.html` in a browser, or play it at https://nicholascraycraft-spec.github.io/4D-gamelet/

You're a pinhole camera in four dimensions. Your film is the 3-sphere of directions
around you, charted into a solid ball: straight ahead at the centre, straight behind
smeared over the boundary. Six views show that ball from every side.

## The game

Reach the green sphere. Every target is the same size, so apparent size is your
range cue. Grey slabs are solid, and a tether keeps you within 7 units of the start.
Sidesteps are slow — turn toward the sphere and fly forward. On arrival you're scored
on efficiency: the direct line to the sphere's surface divided by the distance you
actually travelled (★★★ at 85%+, ★★ at 65%+).

## Keys

| Keys | Action |
|---|---|
| W / S | forward / back |
| A / D, R / F | left / right, up / down |
| Q / E | kata / ana (the fourth direction) |
| ← → ↑ ↓ | yaw, pitch |
| Z / X | roll |
| [ / ] | turn forward into ana |
| N M, K L | the other two 4D turns |

Other scenes: tesseract, two tesseracts one step ana, 24-cell, hypercubic lattice,
Hopf fibration on a 3-sphere.

Needs an internet connection (three.js r128 and fonts load from CDNs).
